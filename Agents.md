# mybudget

* A simple budgeting app for managing personal finances. It allows users to track their income, expenses, and savings goals in an intuitive interface.

* Single page HTML5 app

## Features

* Enter regular and one-off income
* enter regular and one-off expenses
* Balance tracking
* Prediction of future balances based on current income and expenses
* Table view 
* Graph view

## Design
* simple and inuitive interface
* Colors: white, dark blue, light blue, black
* responsive design
